MYM (MoveYourMusic)

Your Spotify and YouTube Music mixes, kept fresh in Apple Music.

MYM mirrors the mixes made for you (Daily Mixes, Discover Weekly, Release Radar, radios, and YouTube Music's own mixes) into your Apple Music library, and keeps them up to date on its own.

How it works

  1. You sign in on Spotify's or YouTube Music's own website, inside the app.
  2. MYM reads your mixes the same way the website shows them to you.
  3. Each song is matched in the Apple Music catalog, in any language.
  4. Playlists are created and updated in your Apple Music library every morning, in the background.

Free, personal, and private

  • Completely free. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases, ever.
  • A personal project. Built by one developer in spare time, because I wanted my mixes in Apple Music. It isn't a business and makes no money.
  • No data collection. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no server. Everything stays on your iPhone, and I never see any of it.
  • Only what you approve. MYM only reads your mixes; the optional Taste sync Likes songs on Spotify only when you review and send them.

Trademarks and respect for the services

  • Spotify, YouTube Music and Apple Music are trademarks of their respective companies. MYM uses their names only to say which services it works with, and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
  • Playlists, song details and cover art belong to their owners and the services. MYM shows them only to you, on your own iPhone, from your own signed-in accounts.
  • MYM never downloads, records, plays or shares music. Songs in your Apple Music library come from Apple's own catalog, under your own Apple Music subscription.
  • You sign in yourself on each service's own page, and you can log out or delete the app at any time.

Works on any iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and is made for iPhone Duo: side by side when open, everything within reach when folded.
