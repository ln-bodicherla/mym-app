MYM (MoveYourMusic)

Your Spotify mixes, kept fresh in Apple Music.

MYM mirrors your Spotify Daily Mixes, Discover Weekly, Release Radar and radios into your Apple Music library. It matches every song, flags the ones it can't find for you to review, and keeps the playlists up to date in the background.

No account, no tracking — everything stays on your iPhone.
