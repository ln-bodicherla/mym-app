Privacy Policy

MYM (MoveYourMusic) · Last updated October 24, 2026 (Taste sync)

Summary

MYM doesn't collect any data. It has no account of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer never receives your information.

What stays on your iPhone

Your Spotify playlist names, songs and cover images, the Apple Music matches, your listening stats, the list of likes MYM sent, your settings and sync history are stored only on your iPhone. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Spotify

You sign in on Spotify's own website inside the app. MYM never sees or stores your password. Spotify's sign-in cookies are kept on your iPhone so the app can read your playlists. MYM reads your mixes and radios. With the optional Taste sync, it can also Like songs and Follow artists on your Spotify account — only the ones you review and send, never automatically — and you can undo every like it sent in Settings. MYM never plays or streams anything. Choosing Log out in Settings removes the cookies.

Apple Music

With your permission, MYM uses Apple's MusicKit to search the Apple Music catalog and to create and update playlists in your library. Playlists you made yourself are never changed. For Taste sync, MYM also reads how often you play the songs it mirrored and which ones you ♥ Love, to suggest Spotify likes; this stays on your iPhone. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Media & Apple Music.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, MYM shows local notifications on your iPhone about syncs. They are created on the device; no push service is used.

Network requests

The app only connects to Spotify (to read your playlists and cover images, and to send the likes and follows you approve) and Apple (to match songs, save playlists and read your loved songs). Those requests are covered by Spotify's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Children

MYM doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
