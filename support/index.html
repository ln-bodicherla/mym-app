Support

MYM (MoveYourMusic)

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

What do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 17 or later (iPhone Duo included), a Spotify account (free or Premium) and/or a YouTube Music account, and an Apple Music subscription with Sync Library turned on (Settings → Music).

Is MYM really free?

Yes. It's a personal, non-commercial project: no ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases, and no data collection. Nothing about you ever leaves your iPhone except the requests to Spotify, YouTube Music and Apple that syncing needs.

Sync says it needs an Apple Music subscription

Adding songs to your library requires an active Apple Music subscription. If you're subscribed, check that Sync Library is on in Settings → Music.

Some songs weren't added

A playlist shows “48 of 50 matched · 2 not on Apple Music” when MYM couldn't find a close enough match, usually because the song isn't in the Apple Music catalog in your country. MYM looks again each time the playlist syncs.

Spotify says I'm not logged in

Open Settings in MYM, tap Log out next to Spotify, then Connect and sign in again.

Background sync didn't run

iOS decides when background tasks run. Make sure Background App Refresh is on for MYM (Settings → General → Background App Refresh). If iOS can't finish a sync, MYM sends a notification; tap it to finish.

How do I delete a mirrored playlist?

iOS doesn't let apps delete playlists. Delete it in the Music app. When a mix is no longer offered, MYM shows it as “No longer recommended · kept in Apple Music” and stops updating it.

Can I sync more YouTube Music mood mixes, or all their songs?

Yes. In Settings → Syncing → YouTube Music, choose which of each mood's mixes sync (Mix 1, every mix, or every mix and the Supermix) and how many songs each keeps.

What does Taste sync send to Spotify?

Only Likes (and Follows for artists you like several songs from) that you review and send yourself. To reverse them, open Settings → Taste sync → Undo likes sent by MYM. Your own likes stay. You can turn Taste sync off in Settings.

How do I remove my data?

Log out of Spotify and YouTube Music in MYM's Settings, then delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else. See the privacy policy.
