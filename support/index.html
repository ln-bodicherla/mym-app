Support

MYM (MoveYourMusic)

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub — include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

What do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 17 or later, a Spotify account (free or Premium), and an Apple Music subscription with Sync Library turned on (Settings → Music).

Sync says it needs an Apple Music subscription

Adding songs to your library requires an active Apple Music subscription. If you're subscribed, check that Sync Library is on in Settings → Music.

Some songs say “needs review”

MYM couldn't find a confident match in Apple Music. Open the playlist, tap Review, and pick the right song or skip it.

Spotify says I'm not logged in

Open Settings in MYM, tap Log out next to Spotify, then Connect and sign in again.

Background sync didn't run

iOS decides when background tasks run. Make sure Background App Refresh is on for MYM (Settings → General → Background App Refresh). If iOS can't finish a sync, MYM sends a notification — tap it to finish.

How do I delete a mirrored playlist?

iOS doesn't let apps delete playlists. Delete it in the Music app; MYM marks playlists that left Spotify as “(removed)” so they're easy to find.

How do I remove my data?

Log out of Spotify in MYM's Settings, then delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else. See the privacy policy.
